Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026. This policy describes what SchemaCraft AI collects when you use schemacraft-ai.site.

Who we are

SchemaCraft AI operates this website. Contact: support@schemacraft-ai.site.

What we collect

  • Audit URLs. When you run an audit, the URL you submit is sent to our server so we can fetch that page. We need the URL to retrieve HTML, headers, robots.txt, and JSON-LD. We do not ask you to create an account for this.
  • Hosting logs. The host (Hostinger) and CDN may log IP address, user-agent, and requested path as part of normal website operation.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not require a login. Pasted JSON-LD, robots.txt and llms.txt content is processed in your browser and is not sent to our server. Audit requests are rate-limited per IP address; the counter is stored temporarily on the server and is not linked to you.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies and does not use a marketing pixel or analytics cookie. Fonts are served from our own domain.

Retention

Server audit requests are processed to return a report. Hosting logs follow the host’s default retention. Emails you send to support are kept as long as needed to answer you.

Your choices

You can stop submitting URLs at any time. For a data request, email support@schemacraft-ai.site.
